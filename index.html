Our policy proposal

We petition Virginia lawmakers to examine a statewide CPS employee integrity-screening framework, potentially including legally authorized polygraph testing, paired with independent evidence audits and a properly empowered Citizen Oversight Committee.

Proposed inquiry topics: deliberate fabrication of evidence; knowing falsification of documents; material misrepresentation; knowing abuse of authority in child removal; and retaliation against protected reporting.

Important: This is a proposed policy, not existing law. Polygraph results, refusals or inconclusive findings do not establish misconduct. Any investigation must use independent corroborating evidence and fair procedures. We support lawful child-protection interventions.

Comparisons with screening in correctional settings are arguments for legislative review, not proof of a constitutional equal-protection entitlement.

Report safely

Immediate danger: call 911. To report suspected child abuse or neglect in Virginia call 800-552-7096 (24/7). This site does not accept complaints, files or identifying details about children. Do not publish confidential CPS records, allegations, children's names, photos or case numbers. Contact an authorized agency or attorney through its secure process.
