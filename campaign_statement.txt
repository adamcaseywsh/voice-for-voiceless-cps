VOICE FOR THE VOICELESS — CPS & FAMILY PROTECTION
PROTECT CHILDREN. PROTECT FAMILIES. STOP RETALIATION.

PROPOSED POLICY: Ask Virginia lawmakers to evaluate a statewide, legally authorized CPS employee integrity-screening framework, potentially including standardized polygraph examinations, independent audit of evidence handling, and an independent Citizen Oversight Committee with properly authorized access to confidential records. No screening program is represented as existing or legally mandatory.

PROPOSED SUBJECT AREAS: Knowing fabrication of evidence; deliberate falsification or material alteration of records; intentional misrepresentations to courts or supervisors; knowingly unlawful child removal actions; retaliation for protected reporting. Questions must be specific, validated for lawful use, and reviewed by qualified independent experts.

SAFEGUARDS: A polygraph result, refusal, or inconclusive examination is not proof of misconduct or an automatic basis for adverse action. Independent review must rely on corroborated evidence, procedural fairness, confidentiality, and applicable labor and civil rights. The proposed oversight committee must have lawful authority, appropriate training, conflict controls, and safeguards for children and employees.

POLICY COMPARISON: If integrity screening is used in some correctional settings, lawmakers should assess whether a comparable lawful accountability framework could improve child welfare oversight. This is a policy comparison, not a demonstrated Fourteenth Amendment equal-protection entitlement.

The initiative supports necessary lawful child protection interventions and does not equate a disputed finding with misconduct. No children's identities, confidential files, or allegations should be posted to the campaign website or public repository.
